# OtoDock Contributor Agreement

Version 1.0, 2026-11-10. Built on the Apache Software Foundation's Individual and Corporate Contributor License Agreements, with the changes Greek law needs and the promises OtoDock makes back.

OtoDock is fair source: every release is licensed under the Functional Source License, Version 1.1, ALv2 Future License (FSL-1.1-ALv2), and becomes Apache-2.0 two years after it ships. This agreement is what lets us include your work in OtoDock, run it, sell it and keep it available. You keep your copyright. You stay free to use your own work for anything else.

Part A is for a person contributing their own work. Part B is for a company whose employees or contractors contribute as part of their job.
