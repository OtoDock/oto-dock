The page header. A title in the display face, a status word, the time of the last update and one main action. It stays at the top while a long page scrolls. Use it on every app with live data. A person then knows at a glance whether what they see is current. The status word says Connecting until the live connection opens, Live while it is open and Reconnecting when it dropped. Without a live connection only the update time shows.
