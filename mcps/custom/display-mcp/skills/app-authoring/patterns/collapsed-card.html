A card that folds. Its whole header is one button. A click, Enter or Space opens and closes it, and a screen reader hears whether it is open. The chevron turns a quarter in 200 ms. Use it for a group a person opens now and then: a day's bookings, a long log, the advanced settings. A control a person must reach stays outside the fold.
