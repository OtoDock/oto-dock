# Breath asset provenance

**CC0 (public domain)** recording from freesound.org — license verified on
the sound page at download time. The shipped file is a derivative:
downmixed to mono, resampled to 8 kHz, trimmed to end at the inhale's
natural energy peak (~0.33s — so the clip crescendos into the speech that
follows the cut), 20ms edge fades, peak-normalized to 0.5 FS (the runtime
`phone_breath_gain` + a rising envelope in `breath.py` scale further).
